The service

Step Showdown lets you run private step challenges, called showdowns, with people you know. Steps come from Apple Health. Your first showdown is free. After that, unlimited showdowns need Premium.

Premium

Premium is available as:

  • Monthly, an auto-renewing subscription
  • Yearly, an auto-renewing subscription with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers
  • Lifetime, a one-time purchase

Prices are shown in the App Store and in the app before you buy, and may vary by country or region.

Purchases are made through Apple and charged to your Apple Account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. If you start the Yearly free trial and don't cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, your subscription starts and you're charged for a year. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in the Settings app on your iPhone (tap your name, then Subscriptions).

Lifetime is a single payment for Premium in the app, with no subscription.

Yearly and Lifetime can be shared with your family through Apple's Family Sharing, if you have Family Sharing set up and sharing turned on for purchases. Monthly can't be shared.

Refunds are handled by Apple under Apple's policies. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Health and safety

Step Showdown is for friendly competition. It isn't a medical device and doesn't give medical or fitness advice. Talk to a doctor before increasing your activity, and walk somewhere safe. You're responsible for your own safety while competing.

Step counts come from Apple Health and the devices you use. They may differ from other apps or devices, and can take time to sync.

Playing fair

  • Don't try to falsify step data.
  • Choose a display name that isn't offensive or pretends to be someone else.
  • Don't misuse invitation links, other people's data or the app's services.

Availability and syncing

Step Showdown depends on iCloud, Apple Health, notifications and your internet connection. Updates can be delayed. Results are saved after a showdown ends, using the steps that have synced by then. Saved results are final.
