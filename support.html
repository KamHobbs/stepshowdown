Need help with Step Showdown?

Common questions

My steps aren't showing. Make sure you granted step access in the Health app under Sources, and that you are signed in to iCloud. Pull to refresh on the Showdown screen.

How do I invite a friend? Open the Friends tab and tap the invite button to share a private link.

How do I restore my purchase? Settings, then Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel? Manage your subscription in your Apple ID settings.
