Privacy Policy

Step Showdown is developed by Kamwana M. Hobbs ("we"). This policy explains what data the Step Showdown app and stepshowdown.app collect, how it's used, and how to remove it.

What the app collects

  • Step count, read from Apple Health on your iPhone and Apple Watch, with your permission. The app reads step count only and saves a step total for each showdown you're in.
  • Your display name and profile color, which you choose.
  • An identifier for your iCloud account, provided by Apple. There's no Step Showdown account or password.
  • Showdown activity: showdowns you create or join (including their dates and time zone), your friend connections, invitation links you create, and reactions you send.
  • Purchases: Apple handles payment, and we never receive your payment details. The app checks with Apple which of your purchases are active.

How it's used

Only to run Step Showdown: showdowns, leaderboards, results, badges, notifications and Premium. We don't use your data for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Where it's stored and who can see it

Your data is stored in Apple's iCloud.

  • Shared records (your display name, profile color, showdowns, step totals, friend connections, invitations and reactions) are stored so that your friends and the people in your showdowns can see them in the app. We can also see shared records, to run and support the app.
  • Private records (your profile, Premium status, badges and other records the app uses to manage your showdowns) are stored in your own iCloud account. We can't see them.

The app also keeps copies on your device so it can work offline.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Step Showdown uses them only for showdown alerts. They're delivered through Apple's notification service.

Support email

If you email support@stepshowdown.app, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply. Our email is provided by Zoho Mail.

This website

stepshowdown.app is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security. This website has no analytics, ads or tracking.

Companies that handle your data

Apple (iCloud, purchases and notifications), Zoho (support email) and GitHub (this website). We don't share your data with anyone else, and we don't use analytics tools, advertising networks or third-party SDKs.

Keeping and deleting your data

Data is kept until it's deleted.

  • Disconnect My Data, in the app's Settings, deletes your profile and display name.
  • To delete your other shared records, email support@stepshowdown.app. Results saved on showdowns you finished with other people stay, because they're part of everyone's results.
  • Private records are stored in your iCloud account and managed by Apple. We can't access or delete them.
  • To stop sharing new data, turn off Step Showdown's Health access or notifications in the Settings app on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes the copies on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date below.
