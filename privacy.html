Step Showdown Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains how Step Showdown handles information through its iPhone and Apple Watch app, website at stepshowdown.app, and support communications.

Privacy at a glance

  • We read step counts only from Apple Health, with your permission.
  • You choose to share showdown totals and standings with people in the same showdown.
  • We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or track you across other apps or websites.
  • You control Health access and can request deletion of your data. Completed showdown results may remain as part of the shared competition history.

Information the app uses

  • Step counts. With your permission, Step Showdown reads step-count data from Apple Health on iPhone and Apple Watch and saves totals relevant to the showdowns you join. The app does not request routes, workouts, heart rate, weight, or other Health data. It does not write to Apple Health.
  • Display name and profile color. You choose these for your in-app identity.
  • iCloud identifier. Apple provides an identifier associated with your iCloud account so Step Showdown can recognize your profile. We do not require a separate username, password, or email address to sign into the app.
  • Showdown and friend activity. This includes showdowns you create or join, dates and time zones, membership, invitations, friend connections, step totals, standings, reactions, badges, and final results.
  • Premium access. Apple handles in-app purchases and subscriptions. The app checks your purchase entitlements with Apple to determine Premium access. We do not receive your payment card details.

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to include in your message.

How information is used

We use this information to create and manage showdowns, send invitations, display step totals and leaderboards, calculate and preserve results, award badges, provide notifications, enable Premium features, and answer support requests.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for cross-app tracking. We do not include third-party advertising or analytics software in the app.

Storage and visibility

Your app information is stored in Apple's iCloud. The app also keeps some copies on your device so it can work offline.

  • Shared information: People in the same showdown can see your display name, profile color, showdown totals, standings, reactions, and results. Your friends can see your display name and profile color. Your friend connections and invitations are used for friend and invitation features. We can access shared information to operate and support the app.
  • Private information: Your private profile, Premium status, and some other app information stay in your own iCloud account. We cannot access that private information.

Showdowns are invitation-based and are not a public social feed. Other participants may nevertheless save or share information they can see in the app. We cannot control what they do with that information.

Health permissions and notifications

You control whether Step Showdown can read Apple Health step counts. You can withdraw that permission in the Health app or your iPhone settings. Doing so stops future access but does not erase step totals already saved in shared showdowns.

If you allow notifications, Step Showdown uses Apple's notification service for showdown-related alerts. You can disable notifications in your device settings.

Support email and website

If you contact support@stepshowdown.app, we receive your email address and message and use them to respond. Our email provider processes those messages only to deliver our email.

Our website at stepshowdown.app uses a hosting provider that may process technical information, including visitors' IP addresses, only to operate and protect the site. We do not add advertising or analytics tools to the website.

Apple provides the Health access, iCloud, notifications, and purchase services used by the app. We do not give personal information to advertising networks or data brokers.

Your choices and deletion

  • Change your profile: You can update your display name and profile color in the app.
  • Leave a showdown: You can leave through the app. Records of participation and information already used to calculate standings or results may remain.
  • Disconnect My Data: This option in the app's Settings deletes your profile information. It does not remove all past showdown activity or results.
  • Request deletion of other shared records: Email support@stepshowdown.app. We will review and act on requests for records we can access and remove, subject to preserving other participants' shared competition history and final results.
  • Private iCloud information: This stays in your Apple account. We cannot access or directly delete it for you.

We retain information as needed to operate Step Showdown and preserve shared competition history. Different records may be retained for different periods, and there is no single automatic deletion date. Completed showdown results and related history may remain after you leave a showdown or disconnect your data because they are also part of other participants' records. Deleting the app removes its locally stored app data but does not necessarily remove data already stored in iCloud.

If you are a parent or guardian with a privacy question or concern about information associated with a child, please contact us at the support address below. Step Showdown does not ask users to provide their birth dates or independently verify their ages.

Policy changes

We may update this policy as the app, website, or our practices change. We will publish revisions on this page and update the date above.
